SSAF: one enquiry, the whole architecture

A visual walkthrough of how a Student Services & Amenities Fee (SSAF) enquiry moves through the system — which component does what, and why a single topic is enough to prove the design. SSAF is proposed as the launch (MVP) use case: high volume, and it touches only one back-end system of record (SIS), so it isolates the architecture without dragging in extra integrations.

Companion to infrastructure.html, decisions-and-rationale.html and managed-foundry-alignment-review.html · grounded in the prototype scenarios (persona: Aisha)

1. Why SSAF for the MVP

“SSAF would work as the MVP use case… Other options considered were Statement of Account and Academic Transcripts, which are also high-volume enquiries but potentially have more complexity. SSAF likely only requires connection to a single system (SIS). SSAF enquiries are high volume across all channels — the current chatbot, livechat, and the standard text responses staff use — giving us a reasonable point for comparison.” — Business framing (BF)

Three reasons it is the right first cut:

  • High volume, one system. SSAF questions flood every channel, yet the only system of record behind them is SIS (via SAP PO). One clean integration to stand up, not five.
  • A built-in baseline. Because SSAF already runs through the chatbot, livechat and staff canned-responses, we have a real before/after to measure against — deflection, accuracy, time-to-answer.
  • It exercises the whole pattern model. The same topic arrives with three different intents — explain, check, act — so one use case demonstrates the classifier, the skills, knowledge grounding and the action/write path. That is the part Charlie could not do.

5. MVP scope — in, and deliberately out

In for the MVP. The SSAF topic across three reactive patterns — Inform-and-Act (“what is it”), Status-Check (“is it paid / why the hold”) and Decide (“defer it to HELP”). One back-end system, SIS via SAP PO. The defer-SSAF Action API with eligibility, confirmation and idempotent write.
Out for now — follow-up. The proactive Nudge (“your SSAF deadline is in 6 days”) is a later release — it needs the outbound-message path, not just SIS. Other high-volume topics — Statement of Account, Academic Transcripts — were considered but carry more complexity and likely more systems; revisit once SSAF proves the pattern end-to-end.
The comparison we get for free. Because SSAF already runs through the chatbot, livechat and staff canned-responses, the launch can be measured against a real baseline — deflection rate, answer accuracy, and time-to-resolution — per the metrics in metrics-catalogue.html.
